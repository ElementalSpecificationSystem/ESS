# Contributing to ESS

Thank you for contributing to the Elemental Specification System. ESS is a community resource and its quality depends on practitioners across the industry — specifiers, architects, engineers, contractors, estimators, and software developers — engaging with its content.

---

## Before You Contribute

Please read the [Charter](CHARTER.md) to understand ESS's governing principles. All contributions must be consistent with those principles, particularly:

- ESS organizes by **work results** — what is constructed, not who constructs it
- No contribution may assert copyright in any portion of the taxonomy
- All contributions are irrevocably dedicated to the public domain or licensed under ODbL

---

## Types of Contributions

### 1. Minor Corrections (Pull Requests)

For small, non-controversial changes, open a pull request directly:

- Typographical errors in section titles
- Formatting inconsistencies in the CSV or JSON
- Errors in the MasterFormat cross-reference

**How to submit:**
1. Fork this repository
2. Make your change in the relevant file
3. Open a pull request with a clear description of what you changed and why
4. A Stewardship Council member will review within 30 days

---

### 2. New Section Proposals (Issues)

Proposing a new section title or a minor extension to an existing division:

**How to submit:**
Open an issue using the following format:

```
Title: [SECTION PROPOSAL] XX XX XX — Proposed Section Title

ESS Number Proposed: XX XX XX
Proposed Title: [Title]
Division: XX 00 00 — [Division Name]
Work Result Description: [One paragraph describing what work result this section covers]
Why This Section is Needed: [Why does this not fit under an existing section?]
Comparable MasterFormat Section (if any): [MF number and title, or "None"]
Supporting Industry Practice: [Reference to standards, specification platforms, or industry usage that demonstrates this work result is commonly specified]
```

The Stewardship Council will review new section proposals and respond within 60 days. Minor additions may be incorporated in the next point release.

---

### 3. Structural Amendments (Issues — Major)

Proposing changes to division structure, renumbering, or organizational logic requires the formal major revision process defined in the Charter:

- Open an issue clearly labeled `[STRUCTURAL AMENDMENT]`
- Provide full rationale and any supporting documentation
- A 60-day public comment period will follow
- Stewardship Council will vote; a two-thirds supermajority is required for adoption

This process exists to protect the stability of the taxonomy for practitioners and software implementors who depend on ESS section numbers remaining consistent between versions.

---

### 4. Implementation Reports

If you have implemented ESS in software, templates, or workflows, please add yourself to [IMPLEMENTATIONS.md](IMPLEMENTATIONS.md) by opening a pull request. This helps the community find conforming tools.

---

## What We Are Not Looking For

- Section proposals that duplicate existing ESS sections
- Proposals that organize by trade discipline rather than work result (e.g., "Electrical Contractor Scope" is not an ESS section)
- Proposals for product-specific sections (e.g., "Brand X Cladding Panel" is not an ESS section)
- Any contribution that introduces a copyright claim into the taxonomy

## Contributor License Agreement

By submitting a contribution to ESS, you confirm that:

1. You have the right to make this contribution
2. You irrevocably dedicate your contribution to the public domain, or where that is not legally available, you license it under ODbL 1.0
3. You understand that no contributor retains copyright in any portion of the ESS taxonomy
4. Your contribution does not reproduce or derive from any copyrighted taxonomy without authorization
